Mathematical Cryptanalysis & Volvelle Kinematics

Formulations for 5-ring concentric rotational offsets, scribal hand classification matrices, and information-theoretic glyph conditional entropy baselines.

1. Net Volvelle Angular Transformation Function

The net cipher transformation angle $\Delta \Theta_{net}$ is computed by summing the discrete rotational offsets of all five concentric rings ($R_1 \dots R_5$) modulo $360^\circ$:

$$\Delta \Theta_{net} = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{5} \omega_i \theta_i \right) \pmod{360^\circ}$$

Where $\omega_i \in \{+1, -1\}$ denotes directional gear parity, and $\theta_1 = \theta_{scribe}$ represents the scribal hand baseline.

2. Scribal Hand Angular Matrix (Davis 2020)

Following Dr. Lisa Fagin Davis (2020), the manuscript's 5 distinct scribal hands map onto specific angular sector baselines $\mathbf{H}_{scribe}$:

$$\mathbf{H}_{scribe} = \begin{pmatrix} \text{Hand 1} & 0^\circ \\ \text{Hand 2} & 72^\circ \\ \text{Hand 3} & 144^\circ \\ \text{Hand 4} & 216^\circ \\ \text{Hand 5} & 288^\circ \end{pmatrix}$$

Each hand's dialectal orthography generates a systematic $72^\circ$ phase shift along the outer alignment ring ($R_1$).

3. Conditional Glyph Entropy Equation

Information-theoretic baseline measuring character predictability across consecutive EVA glyph pairs $(g_i, g_j)$:

$$H(G_n \mid G_{n-1}) = -\sum_{g_i, g_j \in \Sigma} P(g_i, g_j) \log_2 P(g_j \mid g_i)$$

Voynich conditional entropy $H \approx 2.0 \text{ to } 2.4 \text{ bits/char}$ aligns with homophonic substitution / artificial language structures (Preedy 2025; Bowern & Lindemann 2021).

4. Homophonic Key Expansion

Homophonic mapping matrix expanding single plaintext phonemes into multiple EVA glyph variants:

$$\mathcal{M}_{homo}(\alpha) = \{ g \in \Sigma_{EVA} \mid f_{volvelle}(g, \Delta \Theta) = \alpha \}$$

Explains character frequency flattening and line-initial glyph clustering observed across Language A and Language B (Currier 1976).
